An agent-building session

Build your own writing agent.

A personal AI writing partner that turns your raw ideas — a voice memo, a frustration, a half-formed framework — into LinkedIn posts and long-form newsletter articles that sound unmistakably like you. Not generic. Not robotic. Yours.
